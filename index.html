    About this site

    Built like a scrapbook, maintained like a parts bin.

    The visual language is inspired by handmade craft, stitched fabric, and aesthetics from my favorite videogame growing up, LittleBigPlanet. I've taken some creative liberty with some of the styling, but that game was one of the sparks that made me want to be an engineer! What better way to highlight my professional journey so far, than to pay homage to what got me going in the first place.

    Keep in touch!

    You can reach out to me on LinkedIn or GitHub for professional inquiries.

    Made with fabric scraps, copper traces, and static hosting.
